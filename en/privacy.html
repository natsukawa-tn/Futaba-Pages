Privacy Policy

1. Information we collect

The app requires no account, and the developer does not collect personal information such as your name, email address or location.

2. Where your records are stored

Your feeding, diaper, sleep, temperature and growth records and attached photos are stored only on your iPhone and in your own iCloud (Apple's private database). The developer runs no servers and cannot access this data. If you are not signed in to iCloud, records stay on this iPhone only.

3. Sharing with family

People you invite under Family sharing can view, add, edit and delete the shared family's records and photos through Apple's iCloud sharing. Shared data is stored in the iCloud of the person who sent the invitation (the owner). You can stop sharing at any time, and participants can leave with Leave sharing.

4. Photos

Photos attached to records are deleted automatically 90 days after they are added, unless you choose Keep forever. Camera and photo library access is used only when you take, choose or save a photo to Photos.

5. Notifications

Stool color card reminders and reminders for timers left running are created on your device.

6. Usage statistics

To improve the app, anonymous usage signals (such as screens viewed and features used) may be counted with TelemetryDeck. They never include record content or anything that identifies you, such as your child's name, birth date, recorded values, notes or photos.

7. Purchases

Futaba Premium purchases are processed by Apple. The developer never receives your payment details.

8. Face ID

The Face ID lock uses iOS. Biometric data is never sent to the developer.

9. Exporting and deleting your data

Settings > Export data (CSV) exports your records and photos at any time. You can delete records one by one, or all records of a baby with Delete this baby's records. To remove everything stored in iCloud, go to iPhone Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage.

10. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be announced on this page.
