FAQ

How do I share records with my family?

Go to Settings > Family sharing > Invite family to send an invitation by Messages or email. Once they open it, you can see and log the same records together. Both of you need to be signed in to iCloud.

A timer my partner started doesn't appear on my Lock Screen

Live Activities appear on the Lock Screen when the app is opened on that device. Open Futaba once and the timer your partner started will show on your Lock Screen.

Can I log for twins at once?

Yes. With two or more babies, Home has an All column that logs for everyone in one tap. You can add up to four babies.

How long are photos kept?

Photos attached to records are deleted automatically 90 days after they are added. Choose Keep forever, or Save to Photos to keep a copy in the Photos app. Photos that will be removed soon are listed in Settings > Photos.

Will my data move to a new iPhone?

Yes. Records sync automatically to a new iPhone signed in to iCloud with the same Apple ID.

It says my iCloud storage is full

Shared records and photos are stored in the iCloud of the person who sent the invitation (the owner). When storage is full, text records are still saved on the device, but syncing and saving photos stop. Please free up or add iCloud storage.

How do I delete a baby?

In Settings > Babies, choose the baby and tap Delete this baby's records. All of that baby's records and photos are deleted, also on your family's devices, and this can't be undone. To keep the records, use Hide instead.

I want to start over

Delete all data at the bottom of Settings deletes all babies, records and photos and returns to the initial setup. They are also removed from your family's devices, and this can't be undone. If you only joined someone else's family, you leave sharing and this device's settings are reset; the family's records stay in the iCloud of the person who invited you. Your purchases are not affected.

Can I export my data?

Settings > Export data (CSV) exports your records (CSV) and photos as a ZIP. It is free. The PDF report for doctor visits is a Futaba Premium feature.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to iPhone Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions. Premium stays available until the end of the current period.

Premium isn't active on my new iPhone

Try Settings > Restore purchases. Purchases shared through Family Sharing are restored the same way.

How do I change the language?

Settings > Display > Language opens the iPhone Settings page where you can choose 日本語, 简体中文, 繁體中文 or English for this app.
