Disclaimer

This app is a tool for parents to record their child's feeding, diapers, sleep, temperature, growth and more. It is not a medical device. Records and statistics show facts only (counts, amounts, durations, intervals); the app does not evaluate them or give advice on diagnosis or treatment. Record stool color card numbers by comparing with the actual card in your Maternal and Child Health Handbook; colors on screen are only a guide. The Handbook advises seeing a pediatrician promptly if stool color is close to numbers 1–3. Always consult a doctor, midwife or public health nurse about your child's health.
